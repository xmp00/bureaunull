FAQ

Asked before
you ask.

How fixed is a fixed quote?

Fully fixed. The calculator gives an estimate; before any work begins you receive a written quote that locks scope, price and timeline, valid for 14 days. If you change the scope mid-project, the difference is quoted in advance — never invoiced without your approval.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every package includes a support window (30–90 days depending on tier) during which bugs are fixed free. After that, individual edits are quoted per task, or covered by a monthly care plan you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

Do you use WordPress?

No. Every site is hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No themes, no plugin rot, no page-builder bloat — which is also why our sites load in under two seconds and present a far smaller attack surface.

How do payments work?

50% to schedule your build slot, 50% on delivery. Direct bank transfer (SEPA) is standard; cash on delivery is possible and fully declared with a VAT invoice and receipt. Prices are in EUR, VAT 23% where applicable. We do not take card payments.

Why is there no phone number?

Email plus scheduled video calls — everything in writing, nothing lost. No misheard requirements, no "but you said on the phone". A senior engineer replies within 4 working hours.

What happens if you disappear?

If we cannot complete your project for reasons on our side, you receive a 100% refund of everything paid, including the deposit. On full payment you own the final design and content, with source code licensed to you perpetually. Bureau Null is a registered Irish business — full legal details, CRO and VAT numbers on every quote and invoice.

Isn't it expensive?

Compare it with the cost of one stolen client. If a serious enquiry is worth €1,000 to you and your current site quietly loses two a month to a competitor whose site loads faster and looks safer, the "expensive" site pays for itself in a quarter. And the quote is fixed — the number you approve is the number on the invoice.

My cousin can build it — why you?

They probably can build something that looks like a website. What they will not hand you is semantic HTML Google can rank, a 90+ speed score, and a security test by people who break websites for a living. Cheap sites are the most expensive kind — you pay for them twice.

What if I don't like the design?

You approve the design before we build it. Wireframe, then visual design, then build — each stage signed off by you, with structured revision rounds in writing. You never see a finished surprise; you see a series of decisions you made.

Why is the team anonymous?

You hire outcomes and one accountable name: Robert Jurkevich, CEO, signs every quote and invoice. The specialists stay off the marketing page because clients hire results, not headshots. References available on request.
