The written quote and signed agreement — not this website, not the calculator — define final scope, deliverables, timeline and price. Anything not listed in the quote is out of scope.

Our services are directed at businesses. You confirm you act for business purposes; company name and CRO/VAT number are captured in every quote. You expressly request that work begin within 14 days of signature and acknowledge the statutory withdrawal right is lost upon full performance.

All quotes are fixed-price and valid for 14 days. The calculator on this site provides estimates; a written quote confirms the final figure before any work begins.

Each package includes the stated number of revision rounds. Additional rounds are quoted in advance — never invoiced without approval. Deliverables are deemed accepted if no written objections are raised within 14 days of delivery.

02 — Payment Terms

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Structure

50% deposit to reserve your build slot and begin work. 50% on delivery, before final handover or go-live. Work never starts before the deposit clears.

Methods

Direct bank transfer (SEPA) is our standard method. Cash settlement on delivery is available by prior written arrangement. Cash is accepted up to €9,999 (aggregate); amounts above are settled by bank transfer (anti-money-laundering regulations). Every payment — whatever the method — is invoiced, receipted and declared. Prices are in EUR; VAT at 23% is added where applicable.

Late payment

Invoices unpaid after 14 days accrue 2% per month interest plus reasonable recovery costs. Persistent non-payment may result in site suspension after written notice, and IP does not transfer until payment is complete.

Payment disputes & fraud

We keep signed agreements, delivery records, preview logs and correspondence. If you dispute a payment, contest it in writing with reference to the signed delivery records; we recover only documented actual costs of collection. Chargebacks carry a €250 administrative fee plus documented legal and recovery costs. Suspected fraud is reported to the relevant authorities.

Care plans

Monthly plans bill in advance, cancel any time with 30 days' notice. No refunds for partial months already started.

03 — Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Before work starts

Full refund of your deposit minus a €50 administration fee — no questions asked — any time before work begins.

During the project

If you cancel mid-project, you pay pro-rata for completed deliverables (designs, code, content, research) at the quoted project rate. The remainder is refunded within 14 days.

After delivery

Delivered and approved work — including work deemed accepted under the 14-day rule in our Terms — is not refundable. Every package instead includes a support window (30–90 days depending on tier) during which we fix bugs free of charge.

If we cancel

If we cannot complete your project for reasons on our side, you receive a 100% refund of everything paid — including the deposit.

Deposits & scheduling

The deposit reserves a build slot we decline other work for. Once work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable except where we cancel the project ourselves.

Disputes

Refund requests must be made in writing to hello@bureaunull.com and are answered within 5 working days. This policy does not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
