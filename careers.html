Process — four steps, no theatre

How we hire.

A hiring process is a work sample in itself — ours included. Four steps, one week of decision time, and nobody is ever asked to work for free.

/01

Apply

When a role is open, you send a CV and a short form. No cover-letter essay, no personality quiz — evidence over adjectives.

/02

Craft review

Your portfolio, ranks and code are read by the person you'd actually work with — not screened by an HR filter that has never shipped anything.

/03

Paid work-sample

A paid test task, one to two days maximum, drawn from a sandbox — never from live client work, never shipped to production. Your time has a price; we pay it.

/04

Decision + feedback

An answer within a week. Everyone who completed a work-sample gets detailed, honest feedback — whether the answer is yes or no.
